## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- ONLYOFFICE Viewer for files in Bitbucket Cloud repositories: an option of the file page
  that opens the file in ONLYOFFICE Docs, view only, at the commit being browsed.
- Viewing of documents (DOC, DOCM, DOCX, DOT, DOTM, DOTX, EPUB, FB2, FODT, ODT, OTT, PAGES,
  RTF), spreadsheets (FODS, NUMBERS, ODS, OTS, XLS, XLSB, XLSM, XLSX, XLT, XLTM, XLTX),
  presentations (FODP, KEY, ODG, ODP, OTP, POT, POTM, POTX, PPS, PPSM, PPSX, PPT, PPTM, PPTX),
  PDF and other fixed-layout files (DJVU, OXPS, PDF, XPS) and diagrams (VSDM, VSDX, VSSM,
  VSSX, VSTM, VSTX).
- An error screen with a reload button when the app fails to load or cannot reach the
  ONLYOFFICE Document Server.
- The light and dark themes of Bitbucket.
- Translations into English, German, French, Italian, Russian and Spanish.
